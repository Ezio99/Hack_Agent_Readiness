## Secondary behavioural observation (preserved at the user's request; exploratory)
Arm A sometimes **re-verified unnecessarily**, while arm B **consistently followed the minimum-detail-visit path**. In A, all three runs had the answer identified correctly from the listing (including the explicit prices and stock of the other four Home products), yet every run went back to the listing "to confirm" before answering, and two of the three reopened the Woven Basket detail page a second time (overhead +1 in runs 04 and 05; run 01 re-checked the listing only). In B, all three runs followed the same systematic top-down procedure (filter → Cotton Throw → Reading Lamp → Woven Basket → done) with overhead 0 and no revisits. A possible reading is that visible availability invites redundant "confirmation" behaviour, whereas missing information forces a disciplined procedure; an equally plausible one is run-to-run variability or an artefact of the detail page being the only source of the product ID. With n = 3 per arm and one scope this is a hypothesis to examine in the remaining installments (the `navigation_overhead`, `answered_product_page_visited` and revisit counts already capture it), not a finding.
