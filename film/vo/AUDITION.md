# Voice audition script (same three lines for every candidate voice)

Generate each line as a separate clip, with the same settings for every voice (stability ~50 %, similarity ~75 %, style 0–15 %).
Name the clips `<voice>-1.mp3`, `<voice>-2.mp3`, `<voice>-3.mp3`.

1. Act 1, curiosity:
   "This is Larkspur Community College. Beautifully designed. Easy for people to browse. But what happens when an AI agent tries?"

2. Act 2, tension:
   "Three courses checked. Eleven to go. And the budget is running low. So it stops, and answers with what it has: two of the four right courses."

3. Act 5, optimism (the closing vision; final wording to be approved):
   "Agent Readiness. Building the discovery layer for the agentic web."

Judge each voice on: warmth without sentimentality; natural pauses at the full stops; no trailer-announcer emphasis; the same voice
staying credible from curious (1) to tense (2) to optimistic (3).
