## Presenter guardrails

- A single qualification is visible beside the main results. Do not replace “Improvement not established” with a proven-improvement claim. The detail report keeps all original protocol/confidence disclosures.
- The four-stage view is the recorded S1 presentation. Other imports and historical reports retain their full functionality; do not open them during this take.
- The amber event is browser-tool behavior. Agent/output limitations, page observations and proven website causes are not interchangeable. The public S1 asset has zero verified findings; the suggestion is owner-selected.
- The contemporary arms use compat2. The imported historical baseline uses compat1. Preserve that difference in Experiment details; do not pool EXP-C, R1 and S1.
- Task generation is implemented and an earlier version had a real smoke run. Do not claim that S1's four tasks were model-generated or that this validates the whole current autonomous pipeline. Its investigator was imported, not rerun in S1.
- No website images/diffs are fabricated. Only actions with archived execution evidence appear. The exact three-field intervention is documented, but this public asset omits captured pages.
- Prior work and third-party components are disclosed in the pitch inventory. Flask remains offline dev-provider evidence and is not a second successful real-model demonstration.
- Three minutes is the requested draft length. The actual participant form must still confirm submission/video requirements; no video was uploaded by this work.
