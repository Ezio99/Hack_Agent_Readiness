- Muse-powered task proposal is implemented. The real `live-96a6c3ab04` smoke used an earlier generator and exposed goal-relevance gaps; do not imply that this smoke proves the current generator or that it produced S1's tasks.
- Real Muse investigation exists in archived source/replay records. Its hypotheses did not establish a verified finding for this intervention. S1 imports that investigation and does not measure the whole autonomous pipeline anew.
- The stored frozen rule compares the imported compat1 baseline with patched compat2. The headline **contemporary** comparison uses two compat2 arms. Its supplementary frozen-rule comparison is also inconclusive; it is not a replacement verdict.
