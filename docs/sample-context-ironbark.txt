Ironbark Hire Co. is a family-run equipment-hire business in Marrickville, Sydney. Customers are mostly tradies and weekend DIY renovators who need a machine for a day, a weekend or a week, and who usually compare two or three similar machines on daily or weekend rate, whether the machine is free on the dates they need, and the refundable deposit. Cutting tools (tile saws, cut-off saws, chainsaws) are popular for paving and landscaping jobs. Priorities: shoppers should be able to see the hire rate and availability without phoning us, and the booking calendar should be easy to find from the product page.
