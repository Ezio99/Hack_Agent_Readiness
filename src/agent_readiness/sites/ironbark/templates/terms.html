Hire terms and pricing

How our rates, charges, delivery and deposits work. {{ GST }}

Hire periods and rates

  • A hire day is 24 hours from collection or delivery. One day costs the daily rate shown on the product page.
  • Two to six days cost the number of days multiplied by the daily rate, but never more than the weekly rate. Seven days cost the weekly rate.
  • Hires longer than seven days: please call for a quote.
  • Minimum hire is one day unless the product page states a longer minimum.
  • {{ WEEKEND }}

Damage waiver

Damage waiver is optional and costs {{ (WAIVER * 100) | round | int }}% of the hire charge. It covers accidental damage to the equipment but not loss, theft or misuse.

Delivery and collection

We deliver and collect within the Sydney metropolitan area between 7 and 10 am. The fee covers both delivery and collection and is charged once per job.

Each product page says whether delivery is optional or required for that item. Collection from Marrickville is free.

Refundable deposit

A deposit is held on your card when the hire begins and released within three business days of a clean return. The amount for each item is on its product page. The deposit is not a charge and is not part of the hire cost.

Operators and safety

Operators must be 18 or over. Some items need protective equipment or an induction, which is stated on the product page. Safety gear is available in our accessories.

Fuel, cleaning and returns

Petrol machines are delivered with fuel in the tank and should be returned with the tank full. Fuel for 2-stroke tools is not supplied. Concrete mixers and similar items must be returned cleaned or a cleaning fee applies.

Cancellations

Cancel at least 48 hours before the hire starts for a full release of your booking. Later cancellations may be charged one day's hire.
